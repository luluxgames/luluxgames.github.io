Writing & narrative

It starts with
a story.

Scripts and interactive narratives for games, theatre and animation. My Dramaturgy background shapes how I think about character, structure and the audience’s experience.

Final degree project / 2024 / Grade: 9.5 out of 10

Introduction to Video Game Narratives

Research into video game narrative structures, player agency, mechanics and the MDA Framework, connecting dramaturgy with interactive storytelling.

The project also involved Didascalia V.C., an educational VR initiative with Complutense University of Madrid, where I contributed to narrative structure and storytelling for learning.

Animated short

LEFTOVER

Yago is a shy nine-year-old living in a grey coastal town. His companion, Leftover, is a creature made from discarded marine life and forgotten objects. The screenplay explores autism during childhood, loneliness and the need to be understood.
